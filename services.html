FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is a fractional vCISO?

A fractional vCISO is a senior security leader who works with you part time on retainer. They set your security strategy, handle risk, policy, and vendor decisions, and own your security reporting, without the cost of a full-time hire.

How long does SOC 2 or ISO 27001 readiness take?

It depends on where you start. We begin with a gap assessment, then build the controls, policies, and evidence you need. After the assessment we give you a realistic timeline rather than a generic promise.

What is ISO 42001 and do we need it?

ISO 42001 is the management-system standard for artificial intelligence. If you build with or rely on AI, it gives your customers and regulators evidence that you govern your models and data responsibly.

Do you work with teams outside Nigeria?

Yes. HOUSE603 is remote-first and works with teams worldwide.

How does pricing work?

Security services are retainer-based and scoped to your stage and needs. We agree a fixed monthly scope after a short discovery conversation, so there are no surprises.

Can you both build and secure our software?

Yes. We deliver the engineering and digital transformation work and layer security governance on top, so the same team understands how your systems were built.
